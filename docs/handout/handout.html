The Happy Bakers demo

A short guide: what the demo shows, how to explore it, and how it differs from a live system.

Happy Bakers is a bakery we made up to show Neo ERP at work on a business you can picture. It is an all-vegetarian, eggless bakery factory in Anand, Gujarat. It bakes bread, pav, burger buns, toast, khari and puffs, 18 products in all, and supplies 40 retail outlets on four van routes, 10 corporate customers and its own three stores. It has 35 employees and sells about Rs 4 crore a year.

You can do more than look. Post a dispatch sheet, raise a purchase order, record a production run or enter a store's day-end, and the stock, the balances, the costs and the dashboard change at once.

The dashboard: sales, collections, margin, production, stale returns, spend and cash for the dates you choose, and what needs attention today.

Getting started

  1. Open the link on a laptop or desktop. If it asks you to sign in, use the details we sent with it.
  2. You start on the dashboard as Nilesh Patel, the Owner, who can see and do everything.
  3. Pick another person from the top-right corner to see what their role sees: accounts, purchase and stores, production, sales and dispatch, or a store manager. The menu, the figures and the buttons follow the role.
  4. Use the dates at the top of a screen to look at any month since January 2026.
  5. Open Try this in the menu and follow a journey. The eight journeys are on the next page.

Eight things to try

Try this, in the menu, lists these and ticks each step as you post it. The Owner can do every step.

1
Post today's dispatch for a routeEvery outlet on the route gets its invoice. Sales, finished stock and cash move.
2
Buy flour at a higher rateRaise the order and receive the goods. The cost of bread and its margin move.
3
Enter a vendor bill that does not matchThe bill is held because its rate differs from the order. Approve it as the Owner, then pay it.
4
Record a production run with rejectsSee the yield of the run, and the production loss in the month's profit and loss.
5
Supply one of your own storesSend stock, confirm it as the store manager, enter the day-end with expired units, approve the write-off.
6
Enter a large stale returnA return above the limit waits for the Owner. Approve it and read the outlet's statement.
7
Claim an expenseSubmit it as the store manager, approve it, reimburse it. Spend by location moves.
8
Collect from a corporate customerRecord the payment and deposit the cash in the bank. Receivables and the cash book move.

How the demo data works

Test dataHappy Bakers does not exist. Every customer, vendor and employee name is invented, and any likeness to a real business or person is a coincidence.
Books since 1 January 2026The books open that day with opening balances. Every document from then to the day before you open the demo was written by a day-by-day simulation of the business, so the figures are complete and add up.
Today is yoursThe demo takes the day you first open it as its business date and keeps it. That day's dispatch, transfers, receipts and production wait for you to post. On a later day, a line at the top offers a fresh copy dated that day.
Your entries stay with youWhat you enter is saved in your own browser, on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and another browser or device opens its own copy.
Starting againChoose Fresh copy dated today from the top-right menu. It discards what you entered and builds the history again.

The demo and a live system

The demo runs on its own in your browser, so a few things differ from a live Neo ERP.

In the demoIn a live system
ConnectionsSwitched off.Connections to Tally, Zoho Books, banks and GST verification are set up as part of iNeo and NeoX.
Email alertsThe Notification log lists the emails. None is sent.Neo ERP emails approval requests, overdue receivables, bills falling due, low stock and stock near expiry.
UsersSix people to switch between, with no passwords of their own.Each person signs in to their own account and sees what their role allows.
Attendance, leave and tasksLeft out to keep the demo short.Part of Neo ERP.
Earlier historyThe books start on 1 January 2026 with opening balances.Neo ERP starts with your opening balances at go-live. Bringing in 12 to 18 months of history is part of iNeo.
GSTA summary of output and input GST by rate. Nothing is filed.The same summary on your own documents, to check your return against.

The three offerings

Neo ERP This demo

Digitise daily work in one shared system.

  • Finance: receivables, payables and reporting
  • Expenses, vendors and inventory
  • People: records, attendance and leave, and tasks
  • Approvals with email reminders, roles and an audit log
  • Standard reports from go-live

iNeo Adds

Connect outside systems, bring in history and add basic budgets.

  • Agreed connections such as the bank, Tally or Zoho Books and GST verification
  • 12 to 18 months of earlier history
  • Month-on-month and year-on-year dashboards
  • Automation across functions: low stock raises a purchase requirement
  • Basic budgets, with budget against actual

NeoX Adds

Analyse several years, forecast and plan.

  • Analysis across several years of history
  • Statistical forecasting, such as a sales forecast with suggested production and a 30/60/90-day cash projection
  • Integrated budgets and scenarios

In the demo, a locked card marks each place where iNeo or NeoX adds something. Higher tiers lists them.
